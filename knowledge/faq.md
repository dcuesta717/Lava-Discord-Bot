# FAQ — what Lava Bot may answer on Dan's behalf

The away-reply reads this file verbatim. Keep answers short and exact. If a question isn't covered here, the bot says it will get Dan and flags it — it never guesses on money, contracts, drama, health, or personal stuff (see "Never answer").

Owners: edit this in GitHub; it applies on the next deploy. Every away-reply is logged in `#girls-questions` so you can see what was asked and fix the answers.

## Content & posting
- **How do I send you content / where do I upload?** → Your Google Drive folder (link is pinned in your #📚-resources). Raw clips go in `raw`, the editor puts finals in `ready to post`.
- **What should I post today?** → Check your 🎬-reels-copy-board (videos to recreate) and the CONTENT LIBRARY folders; the bot drops your top picks in #💬-general-chat every morning.
- **Can you write me a caption?** → Yes — type `/caption` in your channel with a one-line brief. You get 3 options; tap ✅ on the one you like.
- **What are my numbers?** → `/my-week` in your channel; the bot also posts your numbers every morning at 7 in #🔔-notification.
- **How many times a week should I post?** → The target is in your profile (ask Dan if unsure); as a rule, at least 4 reels a week and every day if you can.

## Lives
- **Going live — what do I do?** → Type `/live-started` in your channel when you start and `/live-ended` when you're done. The bot checks in every 40 minutes.

## Requests from the team
- **What's this content request?** → The brief is in your #📚-resources with a Drive folder to upload into. Only do what you're actually comfortable with — don't force anything just for content.

## Admin
- **Who do I ask about X?** → Dan for anything money/contract/strategy, Marissa for day-to-day content and scheduling, this bot for the routine stuff above.
- **When does Dan reply?** → Usually within a few hours during the day (ET). If it's urgent, say "urgent" and the bot pings him.

## Never answer (always escalate to Dan/Marissa)
- Pay, percentages, payouts, invoices, contracts, exclusivity, leaving the agency
- Disputes with the team or other girls, anything about another girl's numbers
- Mental health, safety, harassment, legal, medical
- Anything you'd need to promise on the agency's behalf (dates, money, deals)
