# How OnlyFans creators win on social media — the playbook the bot works from

Distilled from agency playbooks (Creators Inc, Aruna Talent, TDM, Inrō, SirenCY), 2026 Instagram/TikTok algorithm write-ups, and the top creators' own accounts. This file is injected into the prompts that scout, classify, research and generate ideas. Keep it factual and current; edit it when the market moves.

## The model in one paragraph
Instagram and TikTok are the **discovery engine**; the paid page is the **monetization layer**. Public content is 100 % IG-safe, never mentions the paid platform by name, and exists to do one job: make **men** stop, watch to the end, comment, share to a friend, and tap the profile. Bio → link page → subscribe should take two taps. Reach is bought with consistency (one platform, daily), hooks (first 2–3 seconds), and curiosity (show 80 %, withhold 20 %). "Curiosity, not content, converts." (erasa)

## Who we are trying to reach
- Men, mostly 18–45, in the US / UK / Canada / Australia. English-speaking. The content has to be readable and replicable by our US-based girls.
- They engage with: a girl who looks like she'd talk to them, a skill or hobby they share (golf, gym, cars, football), a question they can answer ("rate my swing", "which one"), a joke that flatters them or teases them, a transition or reveal, a group of girls, confidence with a wink.
- They do NOT engage with (for our purposes): beauty tutorials aimed at women, brand ads, foreign-language content, content made for other markets (different platforms, languages, dress codes, cultural references our girls don't share), inspirational captions, anything that reads as a corporate feed.

## What performs (ranked by what agencies and the algorithm reward)
1. **Short reels, 7–15 s, one idea, hook in frame one.** Over 30 s completion drops ~40 %. Watch time and completion are the top ranking signals; 60 %+ watching past 3 s expands distribution.
2. **Sends/shares** are weighted 3–5× likes for reaching new people. Content a guy sends to his friend ("look at this") is the goal. Saves ~3× likes.
3. **Comment bait that men answer:** "which one", "rate my …", "this or that", "would you…", a question in the on-screen text, a confession men argue with. Reply to comments in the first hour (doubles comment count); pin a funny one.
4. **Niche + femininity contrast:** golf girl, gym girl, car girl, football girl, fishing girl — competence in a male hobby + a feminine look breaks the stereotype and gets shared into male group chats (Grace Charis: golf first, 3.5M IG, paid page downstream).
5. **Personality formats:** GRWM chat, storytime, day-in-life with something withheld, hot takes, "things men do", POV lines ("POV: you found the girl your algorithm was hiding").
6. **Transitions and reveals:** casual → glam, before/after, outfit swaps cut on the beat — visual novelty in the first second.
7. **Collabs / groups of girls:** borrowed audiences convert better than cold reach because trust transfers; group trends and "friend rating" formats.
8. **Street / podcast-mic questions:** asking strangers, "girls explain …", hot-take answers — highly shareable, comment-heavy.
9. **Trending sounds within 24–48 h** of breaking (2–5 day window before saturation); duets/stitches on viral hot takes.
10. **Words on screen** carry the joke or the question; the video is the backdrop. Text must be readable in 1 second.

## Cadence and funnel rules (what the agency tells the girls)
- Instagram: 1 reel/day (2 posts/day grows 60–80 % faster), 5–10 stories/day, link only in the LAST story of a sequence. TikTok: 1–3/day, staggered. Pick one platform to be daily on; cross-post the same clip (no watermarks — watermarked/recycled content gets suppressed; 10+ reposts in 30 days = removed from recommendations).
- Never say the platform's name; never sexual captions; suggestive pose + commercial CTA reads as solicitation and gets the post buried. Swimwear, fitness, lifestyle are allowed; captions describe nothing sexual.
- Hashtags: 5–8 mixed sizes, rotated; none of the banned/thirst tags. Captions short; the hook lives on screen, not in the caption.
- Bio: identity hook (not "model") + proof + one CTA; one link page; same promise everywhere.
- Answer DMs and comments fast; going quiet kills conversion more than any algorithm change.
- Track: completion rate (50 %+ good, 70 %+ viral), shares per reach (1 %+ excellent), profile visits per view (5 %+ strong), link clicks (3–5 % of profile visits).

## What "library material" means for us
A video belongs in the Content Library only if ALL are true:
- made by a **woman creator** for a **male** audience (or a girl group), English-language, Western market (US/UK/CA/AU style, settings, references);
- **IG-safe** (nothing beyond swimwear/fitness; no explicit captions);
- has a **replicable format** — a girl with a phone, her outfit, her friends and her normal locations could film her version this week;
- has a **reason men engage** we can name (a question, a joke, a skill, a reveal, a group, a POV) — not just "she's pretty";
- is not an ad, not a beauty/skincare tutorial, not a couples/family vlog, not celebrity or news content.

## What each folder is for (the lens the classifier and the idea generator use)
- **skits / funny** — the joke is the hook; men share jokes. Self-deprecating > mean. Short setups.
- **dance / transitions** — movement and cuts as the novelty; trending sound within the window.
- **collabs** — 2+ girls, a bit they do together; the group is the reason to watch.
- **pool / beach** — setting is the hook; swimwear is allowed, captions stay clean; add a question or a bit so it is more than a thirst trap.
- **words-on-screen** — the text is the content: confession, question, "when he…", rate-me; men reply to text.
- **big-boobs** — framing/outfit-led within IG rules; still needs a bit, a question or a reveal to be shareable rather than reported.
- **podcast-questions** — mic in hand, strangers or friends, hot takes; comment magnet.
- **golf / gym-girl / car** — hobby + femininity; skill, gear, fits, humor about being the girl in a male space.
- **personality** — talking to camera; she is the content; storytime/GRWM/hot takes; drives DMs and follows more than views.

## Operators worth studying (and what each one is known for)
- **Creators Inc** (LA, large full-service agency; public "honest playbook"): short-form video is the #1 traffic source ("a single reel that travels can do more in a day than a month of consistent posting"); daily posting on ONE platform compounds; creator collabs convert better than cold reach; lives convert followers; press/runway (Miami Swim Week, NYFW, podcasts) drives search; cross-posting to new platforms is "the cheapest growth available"; two-tap funnel (bio link, story pin and pinned post all lead to the same place); answer every inbound within hours; track renewals, not vanity metrics; change one variable at a time.
- **Aruna Talent** (60+ creators, $10M+/yr managed): the cadence numbers above (1 reel/day + 5–10 stories on IG; 1–3/day on TikTok; 7–15 s reels; hook in 2 s), the 5-story link sequence, 5–8 rotated hashtags, "comment [keyword]" DM funnels, never name the platform, backup accounts.
- **Owen Lynch (@owenllynch)** — young agency owner (reported ~$1M/month agency cash flow, ~785k followers of his own), manages Camilla Araujo among others; teaches "OFM" publicly. What his roster demonstrates: attention is the product and it is bought with **content volume and personality**, not polish; **mainstream crossover collabs with big male creators** (Camilla's MrBeast-era exposure, streamer appearances, podcasts) create the spikes; comedic skits, bait-the-comments bits and relationship/dating humor keep men arguing in the comments between spikes; the paid page stays entirely downstream of a glossy, IG-safe public feed (reported $1.5–2M/month at peak). Lesson for our girls: one repeatable bit + a bigger creator to borrow an audience from beats random posting.
- **Top niche creators** (Grace Charis — golf, 3.5M IG): the niche is the hook, femininity is the contrast, the paid page is never the pitch.
