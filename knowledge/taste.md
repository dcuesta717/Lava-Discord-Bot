## The common thread
He saves short-form skits and POV setups under 15 seconds that create immediate comment bait through relatable relationship or dating humor. Nearly every save features a solo girl or small group talking directly to camera or acting out a POV scenario with on-screen text that frames the joke. The tone is playful, self-aware, and conversational—never polished or scripted-feeling—shot casually in cars, at night outside, or minimal indoor settings.
