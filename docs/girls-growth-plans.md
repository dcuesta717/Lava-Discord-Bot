## What each girl films this week (depth = formats, not more shoots)

- **Morgan** — post daily, not 3×/week; every bikini/pool clip gets one line of text and a question ("rate the dive", "pool or
  beach?"); one flexibility reel a week (her split post proved it); one group reel with Leah/Bryce/Desy per shoot day (they are
  already in the same room); a mic-in-hand question at the next swim-week event. Study: summerxiris, neonrose.official.
- **Bryce** — keep 7/wk but shift from reposting the photographer's cut to her own 10–15 s version with a question; one skit a
  week from her Content House list (10 are waiting on her board); boat/runway clips framed as "would you…" bits.
  Study: juliafilippo_, kyla.doddsss.
- **Leah** — turn the photo dumps into talking reels: "Hot Girl Survival Tips" weekly (keep the series name), a 60–120 s
  storytime or Q&A twice a week (law-grad hot takes, horse-girl stories, "princess rules"), a mic question at shoots. Her
  comments are already fine; video will bring the reach. Study: taylorwatson, kyla.doddsss.
- **Desy** — one 6–8 s direct ask per day ("sorry for interrupting…" is a format, repeat it), long-form stays weekly, and the
  group content with Morgan/Mia tagged as collabs so both audiences see it. The playbook, not the subject matter, is the
  advice here; what she talks about in her long-form is hers. Study: alesalaslopez for the asks.
- **Kalena** — cosplay every week (it is her outlier), always with "comment your fav …"; runway walks as reels with a hook
  line; the brand-code posts are fine but they are not what grows her. Study: lisa.mancinerh, akiracozer.
- **Madi** — video, daily, 8–15 s: fit checks, "does this look good on me?", braces as a recurring bit, text on every clip;
  keep the question captions (they are her best comments). She has the vibe Dan wants and is posting the least.
  Study: vivylue (Dan's pick), alesalaslopez.
- **Naomi** — do not change the formula; add two group reels a week with a bigger girl in the crew (borrowed audience is
  the only thing a 6k account cannot make alone), cross-post to TikTok, use trending sounds. Study: arikytsya.
- **Natt** — a 30-day habit: one filler reel a day (mirror, bedroom, "babygirl" text bits), one duo reel a week with the
  brunette friend; question captions. Study: kyla.doddsss, vivylue.
